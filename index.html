About Me

Hi, I'm Sophia.

I'm a multidisciplinary designer based in Frankfurt, specialising in UX/UI design, motion graphics, 3D visualisation and spatial design. I love turning complex ideas into clear, compelling visual experiences.

My work lives at the intersection of technology and aesthetics — whether it's designing an intuitive interface, animating a brand story, modelling a product in 3D, or shaping a physical space that tells a narrative.

I'm always excited to collaborate on projects that push creative boundaries. Let's build something great together.
